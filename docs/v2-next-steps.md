# V2 Preview Next Steps

VATSIM Radar v2 Public Preview has come to an end. 

That means a few things.

1. Access to Next will be kept for a week (until 02 Aug or so) and then closed for Early Adopters and Patreon Supporters only
2. All your data on Next will be lost, except you are from above category

In order to migrate your data:
1. For Settings: 
   1. Open Settings → Save or Load → Presets Management
   2. Open each preset and click Export button
   3. On Production, open same page and click on "Import from File"
2. For Favorite Lists (Friends):
   1. Open Settings → Favorite Lists
   2. Open each list and click Export button
   3. On Production, create according list or open json you download to an existing list

Thanks every much for testing! Most active members of Closed Beta program will be transferred onto Early Adopters. 

For others, new opportunities to join Early Adopters will open closer to v2.1 testing.
